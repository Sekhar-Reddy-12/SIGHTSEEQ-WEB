Support

Answers to the things people ask us most often.

How long is the warranty?

Every SIGHTSEEQ watch ships with a two-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects, the display and the battery. Water damage beyond 50 metres and accidental drops are not covered, but we do offer a discounted repair programme.

Does it work with iOS and Android?

Yes. SIGHTSEEQ OS pairs with iOS 15+ and Android 10+ through the SIGHTSEEQ companion app. Calls, notifications, activity tracking and contactless payments are available on both platforms.

What is the battery life?

Up to 36 hours in standard use and up to 72 hours in low-power mode. A 20-minute charge gives you roughly a full day of use.

Can I return it?

You have 30 days from delivery to return an undamaged watch for a full refund. Return shipping is free within the EU, UK and US.

About us

SIGHTSEEQ started in a garage in 2018 with a simple frustration: smartwatches that demanded attention instead of giving it back. We set out to build the opposite — a watch that shows you what matters, then gets out of the way.

Today we are a team of 42 engineers, designers and support specialists working from Rotterdam and Lisbon. Every component is specified in-house, every unit is water-tested before it leaves the line, and every software update is supported for at least five years after purchase.

We believe good hardware should be repairable, good software should be quiet, and good companies should be easy to reach.
